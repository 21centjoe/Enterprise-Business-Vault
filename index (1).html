    Freshness & Integrity Cycle

    On a real interval, each encrypted asset is extracted (decrypted with the session's Master Key), checked (CRC32 of the plaintext recomputed and compared against the value stored at encode time — on top of the authentication tag AES-GCM already checks on every decrypt), and then cold-stored again (re-encrypted with a freshly generated IV, with a fresh embedded copy of the current passphrase/coin wrap blocks, and rewritten to your chosen storage location).

    On "industry standard": there isn't one single universal number every industry body mandates for data-freshness/integrity re-checks — it varies by data type and risk tolerance (common reference points range from daily integrity scrubs to weekly/monthly backup verification). The default below (24 hours) is a reasonable, commonly-used convention for this kind of check-in cycle, and it's configurable — this is not being presented as a fixed law.
